FAQ

Questions we get every week

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes — all plans are designed to be upgrade paths. Moving from Starter to Business, or from Essential to Standard support, is a straightforward conversation. We migrate everything on your behalf.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The price you see is the price you pay. Add-ons are fixed-cost line items quoted before any work begins. We don't charge by the hour for out-of-scope requests without telling you first.

What's included in "post-launch support"?

Bug fixes, minor text or image changes, plugin or library updates, and basic performance checks for the duration of the support window. Major new features or structural redesigns fall outside the support window and are quoted separately.

How does the IT support contract work?

Support packages run monthly. We start with an environment discovery session, agree on the scope and SLA, then take ownership. You get a monthly report and a quarterly review call included in all Priority and Enterprise plans.

Do you host the website after delivery?

Yes — we can host and maintain the site on your behalf on AWS, Azure or DigitalOcean, or hand over a fully documented deployment to your own infrastructure team. Hosting is quoted separately from the development package.

What if my project doesn't fit a standard package?

Get in touch and tell us what you're building. We scope every enterprise and unusual project from scratch — there's always a way to structure it fairly for both sides.

Not sure which plan fits?

Book a free 20-minute call — we'll recommend a plan based on your actual environment, not a sales target.
