Our Story

Built by engineers who got tired of unaccountable IT vendors.

NivioIndia Technologies started with a straightforward idea: businesses deserve technology partners who treat their systems like their own. No ticket queues that vanish into a black box, no rotating support staff who don't know your stack.

Today we place and manage engineers across network support, cloud, cybersecurity, infrastructure, and development — for companies in banking, healthcare, manufacturing, and beyond.

  • Engineers matched to your specific stack and scale
  • Transparent reporting and real SLA accountability
  • Flexible engagement models — staffing, projects, or managed services
